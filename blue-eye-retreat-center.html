Nestled in the quiet beauty of Blue Eye, Missouri, our retreat center sits right above Table Rock Lake, offering breathtaking views of the water and surrounding Ozark hills. This peaceful setting provides the perfect escape from the busyness of everyday life, where guests can enjoy the stillness of nature, watch the sunrise over the lake, and take in the calming presence of God’s creation. With its serene atmosphere and natural beauty, our center is a place designed to refresh the soul and renew the spirit.

At Blue Eye Retreat Center, we believe rest is essential, and our mission is to provide a space where people can truly step away and find it. Whether it’s through quiet reflection, time in prayer, or simply enjoying the outdoors, guests are invited to slow down and embrace the peace this unique setting offers. Overlooking the sparkling waters of Table Rock Lake, our retreat center is more than just a destination—it’s a sanctuary where hearts are restored, minds are calmed, and lives are strengthened for the journey ahead.
