OUR AFFIRMATION OF FAITH:

There is one true God who exists eternally in three persons, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, all being equal in deity, power, and glory. God created the world and made man in His own image. 

The sixty-six books of the Bible are the inspired, inerrant Word of God. 

Satan, angel who rebelled against God, was cast out of heaven,and works to establish a culture of darkness and evil on earth. 

Man, of his own free will, chose to disobey God, resulting in our need for a savior. 

Jesus Christ, God's only Son, was sent to earth to redeem and restore all who would confess their sin and believe in the Lord Jesus Christ for salvation, through His death and resurrection. 

The Holy Spirit, sent by Jesus when He rose to heaven, convicts the world of sin, empowers and guides followers of Christ, conveys spiritual gifts, nurtures the Fruit of the Holy Spirit in the believer’s life, and connects us with our Heavenly Father.

Every man, regardless of race or rank, who receives the Lord Jesus Christ by faith is born into the family of God and receives eternal life. 

God empowers and utilizes the church to fulfill His redemptive purposes on the earth, including: preaching the Gospel to all nations, and ministering to the poor through sacrificial giving and practical service. 

The Lord Jesus Christ will return one day to receive His own. Believers will enjoy eternal glory and fellowship with Him and unbelievers will go to eternal punishment. 

Man’s chief end in life is to honor and glorify Almighty God. Personal salvation is a means to this end. 

God wonderfully and immutably creates each person a male or female. Rejection of one’s biological sex is a rejection of the image of God within that person. 

The term “marriage” has only one meaning: the uniting of one man and one woman in a single, exclusive union for life. God intends sexual intimacy to occur only between a man and a woman who are married to each other. Any form of sexual immorality is sinful and offensive to God. 

*Our affirmation of faith does not exhaust the extent of our beliefs. The Bible itself, as the inspired and infallible Word of God that speaks with final authority concerning truth, morality, and the proper conduct of mankind, is the sole and final source of all that we believe. For purposes of Freedom Fire’s faith, doctrine, practice, policy, and discipline, our staff and board of directors are the final interpretive authority on the Bible’s application.

MEGAN & JT BATEY:

Megan & JT met in January of 2019 and were married in September of 2021. Megan grew up going to Freedom Fire as a child and even gave her life to Jesus at one of their Friday night outreaches at Wayne Miner. She currently works full-time for Freedom Fire as a youth director. JT grew up going to church in Northeast before feeling a call from God in 2017 to plant a church in Kansas City. The Batey's love Jesus and love downtown Kansas City. They exist to tell everyone they can the Good News about Jesus.
