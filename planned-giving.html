Planned Giving

Planned giving is an opportunity to carry your generosity forward. A future gift can help Freedom Fire continue building relationships, creating opportunity, and sharing Christ’s love in Kansas City for years to come.

Explore Planned Giving

Ways to make a lasting gift

There are several thoughtful ways to include Freedom Fire in your plans. Explore the options below, then use the resource hub to find details that fit your family and goals.

01

Donor-Advised Fund

Recommend a grant from your donor-advised fund to support Freedom Fire’s work and leave a meaningful legacy.

Learn more →

02

Retirement Assets

Consider directing a portion of an IRA or other retirement account to Freedom Fire as part of your charitable giving plan.

Learn more →

03

Life Insurance

A life insurance policy that no longer serves its original purpose may become a generous future gift to the ministry.

Learn more →

04

Other Assets

Non-cash assets such as property, business interests, collectibles, or cryptocurrency can also make an impact.
