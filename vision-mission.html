VISION & MISSION STATEMENT

Purpose Statement:  Freedom Fire is a Christian missions organization dedicated to the advancement of God’s Kingdom and love in the urban core of Kansas City.

Vision:  Our vision is to raise up disciples of Christ from the youth of the inner city.  They will become active, healthy citizens of God’s Kingdom – some of whom will become the next leaders of Freedom Fire, while others take roles of influence in other ministries, churches, organizations, and businesses.  All of these youth will be grounded in the Word, prayer, church, and witnessing.

Mission Statement:  Our mission is strengthening the people of the inner city by equipping them to guide the community spiritually, economically and socially. 

Ministry Philosophy:  We are building purposeful friendships with young people by:  

  • Modeling and experiencing God’s love by valuing the individual through friendships

  • Equipping with skills and knowledge that will enable them to make a positive impact in the community.

  • Living out the spirit of John 15:15 “I have called you friends . . .”
