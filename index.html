TO SEE GOD TRANSFORM LIVES IN THE URBAN CORE.

PURPOSE STATEMENT:

Our vision is to raise up disciples of Christ from the youth of the inner city.  They will become active, healthy citizens of God’s Kingdom – some of whom will become the next leaders of Freedom Fire, while others take roles of influence in other ministries, churches, organizations, and businesses.  All of these youth will be grounded in the Word, prayer, church, and witnessing.

Freedom Fire is a Christian missions organization dedicated to the advancement of God’s Kingdom and love in the urban core of Kansas City.

VISION:

mission statement:

Our mission is strengthening the people of the inner city by equipping them to guide the community spiritually, economically and socially.
