What to look for

Compare the shape of the letters, the boldness of the headings, and where lines wrap. These are alternatives, not identical copies. Jost is used for the replacement buttons in every option.

All four open-source fonts are stored locally with their licenses. They need no Adobe or Google connection. Figtree and Jost are now in the website’s assets/fonts folder; the logo artwork is unchanged.
